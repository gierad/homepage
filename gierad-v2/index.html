About

I'm a Computer Scientist and a Senior Engineering Manager at Apple. I lead the Perception and Interaction Engineering organization under Machine Intelligence Neural Design (MIND) at SWE AIML. We innovate and build differentiating experiences through research and development in multimodal machine learning with vision, audio, motion, and other sensors. We also work on ML innovations that bridge that gap between SWE and Foundation Models. Applications of our work include ambient intelligence, wearables, and AI-driven interactive experiences.

I am currently attending Harvard Business School as part of their executive education program, joining the Harvard Alumni community in 2026.

Prior to Apple, I completed my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University's School of Computer Science, where I was a founding member of the Future Interfaces Group. Prior to Apple, I was a Google Ph.D. Fellow, a recipient of the Fast Company Innovation by Design Award, a Disney Imagineer, and a recipient of ten Best Paper Awards and Nominations at premier venues in human-computer interaction. I'm also an Affiliate Professor at Carnegie Mellon University's School of Computer Science and Human-Computer Interaction Institute.

Features Shipped

We work closely with teams across Apple. Here are select features and experiences where we've made contributions:

Foundation Models Framework

In collaboration with SWE, Foundation Models framework allows developers to build on Apple Intelligence to bring users new experiences that are intelligent, available when they’re offline, and that protect their privacy, using AI inference that is free of cost. Includes Guided Generation, Tool calling, and more. iOS 26, watchOS 26, tvOS 26, macOS Tahoe.

Xcode with ChatGPT

In collaboration with SWE, enable Xcode built-in support for ChatGPT, allowing developers to connect LLMs directly into their coding experience to write code, tests, and documentation, iterate on a design, fix errors, and more. Developers can also use API keys from other providers or run local models on Apple silicon, to use the best model that suits their needs. Xcode 26.

Head Gestures for AirPods

In collaboration with CoreMotion, Siri, Intelligent System Experiences, and AirPods Firmware: machine learning models and algorithms for head gesture detection, enabling hands-free Siri Interactions on AirPods. AirPods 2nd gen+, iOS 18, watchOS 18, and macOS Sequioa.

Apple Intelligence: ChatGPT Integration

In collaboration with SWE, Siri, and OpenAI: part of a cross-functional team enabling system-wide integration and API support for Apple Intelligence and ChatGPT, including on-device and private cloud compute interfaces. iOS 18, iPadOS 18, and macOS Sequioa.

Apple Intelligence: Writing Tools and Personal Search

In collaboration with SWE Input Intelligance: part of a large cross-functional team working on Apple Intelligence for Writing Tools and Personal Context Search, specifically on model safety integration. iOS 18, iPadOS 18, and macOS Sequioa.

Apple Intelligence: Xcode Assist

In collaboration with SWE Xcode Teams: part of a large cross-functional team working on Apple Intelligence for Xcode, specifically on model safety integration for predictive code completion and Swift Assist. macOS Sequioa.

Vision Pro

In collaboration with Video Engineering: part of a large cross-functional team making contributions to research and development in hand input and interactions, including hand-object interactions, synthetic data, gesture customization, and ML model optimizations.

StandBy Mode

In collaboration with the Design Team: early-stage explorations on sensing, perception, and interaction techniques for StandBy mode in iOS.

Time in Daylight

In collaboration with Health, Visual Experiences, and Core Motion: early explorations and algorithm support for time in daylight on the Watch for Vision Health. Recently announced at WWDC 2023.

Temperature and Humidity Sensing

In collaboration with Home Hardware, Home Software, Thermal Design, Hardware QA, Sensing Hardware: machine learning models and self-heating correction algorithms on the HomePod and HomePod Mini for temperature and humidity sensing.

Crash Detection

In collaboration with Core Motion: machine learning models and algorithms on the watch and phone for vehicular detection, serving as an early stage classifier for Crash Detection. Apple Watch Series 8+, Apple Watch Ultra, iPhone 14+.

Always-On Display

In collaboration with iPhone Hardware, Core OS, Core Motion, and Core Bluetooth: machine learning models and algorithms to support the Always-On Display, specifically power optimizations for suppression that levarage the watch-phone ecosystem. iPhone 14 Pro/Max.

Fitness: MultiSport

In collaboration with Core Motion and Health Technologies: sensor fusion models for multimodal workout experiences. Automatic detection of indoor/outdoor swimming, cycling, and running in any sequence, including transitions. watchOS 9, Apple Watch Ultra action button integration.

Fitness: Cycling

In collaboration with Core Motion and Health Technologies: shipped algorithms and models for cycling workout detection and auto- pause/resume, powering Fitness and Fall Detection features. Apple Watch Series 6+

Hand Gestures for Assistive Touch

In collaboration with Accessibility and Human Interface Design. Gesture interactions for Accessibility on the Watch. Assisted in feature productization.
Apple Watch Series 6, SE.

Health: Handwashing

In collaboration with watchOS Software and Health Technologies. Developed pre-COVID. Conceptualized, prototyped, built sensing + machine learning algorithms, and shipped the feature into production. Apple Watch Series 4+.
